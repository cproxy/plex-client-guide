# Roku Ultra + Plex

Roku's current Ultra advertises 4K, HDR10+, Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, with Wi-Fi 6 and 10/100 Ethernet.

For Plex remux libraries, the main limitation is format flexibility rather than basic HDR support:

- NT-Lists testing does not show TrueHD or DTS-HD MA support on the tested older Ultra entry.
- Plex forum guidance continues to document PGS subtitles causing burn-in/video transcoding on Roku.

For users who keep full UHD/Blu-ray PGS subtitles and lossless audio, Roku is therefore a less direct-play-friendly endpoint than Shield/CoreELEC-class clients.
