# Google TV Streamer / Chromecast with Google TV / onn. 4K Pro

These devices share the Android TV/Google TV Plex client family, but their codec/output hardware differs.

## Google TV Streamer 4K

Google documents 4K60, HDR10, HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Vision, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus and Dolby Atmos, plus built-in gigabit Ethernet. Google does not document TrueHD/DTS-HD/DTS:X passthrough in its public specification.

Source: https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/3046409

## Chromecast with Google TV 4K

Google documents 4K60, HDR10/HDR10+/HLG, Dolby Vision and Dolby audio passthrough. Ethernet requires an adapter.

Source: https://support.google.com/chromecast/answer/3046409

## onn. 4K Pro (2026)

Walmart's current 2026 product page lists Dolby Vision, Dolby Atmos, 32GB storage, 3GB RAM, Wi-Fi 6 and wired Ethernet. Exact Plex behavior for lossless audio and Dolby Vision profiles needs controlled testing, so this guide intentionally leaves those fields Unknown.

Source: https://www.walmart.com/ip/18382213962

## Current Plex caveat

The 2026 Android TV Plex client has reports of Dolby Vision 23.976/24 content failing to invoke refresh-rate switching on all three device families.

Source: https://forums.plex.tv/t/new-experience-on-android-tv-refresh-rate-switching-does-not-work-for-24-fps-dolby-vision/941946
