02 // PLAYBACK DIAGNOSTICS

If a title buffers or transcodes

Plex may need to adapt a video, audio track, or subtitle for your device. Try these quick checks.

  1. Check quality. On your home network, set Home or Local quality to Original or Maximum.
  2. Check audio and subtitles. Try another audio track. Test once with subtitles off, or use a text subtitle such as SRT.
  3. Compare players. Try the same title in another Plex app. If it still transcodes, tell the server owner your device, app, title, audio track, and subtitle choice.

Direct Play sends the file as it is. Direct Stream changes its container while keeping compatible video and audio intact. Transcode converts media for compatibility. Plex playback overview >>
