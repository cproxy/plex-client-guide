What Plex is doing

Direct Play is the simplest path.

The player receives the media as it is. Direct Stream changes the container around compatible video and audio, without re-encoding the picture. A Transcode adapts something the player cannot use as sent.

If something transcodes

Try the small checks first.

A single title can behave differently because of its audio, subtitle, file format, or the app playing it.

  1. 1

    Check that local or home quality is Original or Maximum.

  2. 2

    Try another audio track. Turn subtitles off once to compare, then try a text subtitle such as SRT.

  3. 3

    Play the same title in another Plex app. Browser and TV apps may support different formats.

  4. 4

    If you contact the server owner, include the device, Plex app, title, audio track, and subtitle choice.
